## **2. About the Platform**

Sliceal is a platform for fractional ownership of Dubai real estate
through digital tokens recorded on a distributed ledger. Eligible
investors can acquire fractional ownership interests in listed
properties, and eligible property owners can offer properties for
tokenization and sale through the Platform. The Platform facilitates
onboarding and identity verification, property listing and verification,
investment orders, portfolio tracking, rental income distribution, and
transaction management. Sliceal operates within the regulatory framework
of Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA) and works with the
Dubai Land Department (DLD) for property registration.
