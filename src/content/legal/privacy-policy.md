## **1. Introduction**

This Privacy Policy explains how Sliceal collects, uses, shares, and
protects personal data when you use our website, mobile application, and
services. We process personal data in accordance with UAE Federal
Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) and
other applicable regulations.

## **2. Information we collect**

Personal identification:

- Name, email address, and phone number

- Date of birth and nationality

- Government-issued ID details (including Emirates ID or passport),
  collected during identity verification

Financial information:

- Bank account details

- Transaction history and, where required, source-of-funds information

Technical information:

- IP address, device and browser details, and usage data

Blockchain data:

- Wallet addresses and token transactions

## **3. How we use your information**

- Identity verification (KYC/KYB) and anti-money-laundering compliance

- Processing investment orders, transactions, and rental income
  distributions

- Account management, support, and service communications

- Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations, including
  reporting to regulators where required

- Platform security, fraud prevention, and service improvement

- Marketing communications, which you can opt out of at any time

## **4. Legal bases**

We process personal data where it is necessary to perform our contract
with you, to comply with legal and regulatory obligations, for our
legitimate interests in operating a secure platform, or with your
consent where required.

## **5. Data sharing**

We share personal data only where necessary, with:

- Licensed identity-verification (KYC) providers

- Licensed tokenisation, custody, and banking partners, and payment
  processors

- Regulators and government authorities, including VARA and the DLD,
  where required

- Professional advisers under confidentiality obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

## **6. Blockchain data**

Token ownership and transactions are recorded on a distributed ledger.
Records on the ledger are immutable: wallet addresses are pseudonymous,
but once recorded they cannot be altered or erased. Rights to correction
and deletion therefore apply to data we hold off-chain.

## **7. Data security**

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to
protect personal data, including encryption in transit and at rest,
access controls, and continuous monitoring. No method of transmission or
storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security;
we work to protect your data using recognised industry standards.

## **8. Data retention**

We retain personal data for as long as needed for the purposes it was
collected and as required by law and regulation. Identity-verification
and transaction records are retained for the statutory period after your
account is closed.

## **9. Your rights**

- Access the personal data we hold about you

- Request correction of inaccurate data

- Request deletion, subject to legal and regulatory retention
  requirements and the blockchain limitations described above

- Object to or request restriction of certain processing

- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact support@sliceal.io. We will respond
within the timeframes required by applicable law.

## **10. Cookies**

We use cookies to operate the Platform, remember preferences, and
understand usage. You can manage cookies through the cookie settings on
our website and your browser settings.

## **11. International transfers**

Some of our service providers process data outside the UAE. Where
personal data is transferred internationally, we apply safeguards
consistent with the PDPL.

## **12. Children**

The Platform is not directed at individuals under 18, and we do not
knowingly collect their personal data.

## **13. Third-party services**

The Platform integrates with third-party services that have their own
privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies, as they
govern how those providers handle your data.

## **14. Updates to this policy**

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be
notified through the Platform, and the "Last updated" date above will
always reflect the current version.

## **15. Contact**

Privacy questions or requests: support@sliceal.io.
